Walk into every negotiation knowing the best price you can get.

The engine checks what hospitals across the network have paid, what competing vendors have offered, and the best price on record, then gives you the price to ask for.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in and ask

    Type a question like "best price for ceftriaxone from Medisindo", or use the buttons: one product and vendor, or the renewal calendar.

  2. 2

    Get three price options

    Stretch to open with, Target to win, Fallback to hold. Each one beats past internal benchmarks and competitors, and shows the proof.

  3. 3

    Negotiate and track it

    Negotiate gives you a plan. Log the vendor's offers and move each renewal along the board until it's agreed, and watch the savings add up.

Good to know

  • Negotiate one principal at a time. Negotiations follows Template_Nego step by step: build the item list from POs, the formulary and the MOU, read the principal's RFQ back, and let the engine flag typing slips, unit mix-ups and price increases before anything is agreed.
  • Everyone can see everything and update renewal progress. Admins also upload price lists, records and documents, and tune the engine.
  • Prices are per clinical unit (one test, one piece, one vial) and also shown in the vendor's pack, so a box of 50 is never compared with a single.
  • The walk-away price is only a proposal. It needs sign-off from someone other than the negotiator before the engine will say accept, push or walk.
  • Amounts are in rupiah: Rp 9.905 per unit, Rp 539,6 Mn or Rp 5,36 Bn a year (Mn = million / juta, Bn = billion / miliar).
